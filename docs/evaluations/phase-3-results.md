## Failure and provenance record

The manager retained eleven real tasks: six produced valid proposals, four failed and one was cancelled. These mixed tasks are not an end-to-end generation-success cohort. Failed attempts include the rejected legacy permission request, two invalid reuse plans and discovery v1's invalid source references. A partial recording remains retained; its completeness question was not silently removed.

The first real turn exposed deprecated `readOnly.access`; the adapter now selects a denied-filesystem named profile and checks its availability. AST extraction now recognizes the existing `test as base` fixture; the output schema/instructions require exact indexed reuse names. Cancellation races and split UTF-8 characters have focused regressions. No business assertion was weakened.
